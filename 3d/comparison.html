FAMILY HOUSE, the housing society: 3D comparison

Architect's original vs Plan 2 (bridge in the middle) vs Plan 5 (bridge on the right wall). The same 14 views were rendered for each design, from the same camera positions (except the first floor interior view, which is placed where each design has a good view over its open space). These are massing models built from the measured plans: walls, openings, floors, double-height voids, stairs, railings and simple furniture for scale. Colours, finishes, window sizes and the front elevation are indicative.
