# House plans: three layout options

A 5-bedroom, two-storey joint-family house. The architect's original layout is compared with two owner revisions.

**Website:** https://zain10k.github.io/house-plans/

| Option | Idea |
|---|---|
| A: Architect's original | Double-height space as a front-to-back strip on the right side |
| B: Plan 2 | Kitchen and dining swapped; full-width double-height space with a bridge in the middle; floating L-stair |
| C: Plan 5 | As Plan 2, with the bridge along the right wall so nothing crosses the open space |

## Contents
- `3d/viewer/`: interactive 3D viewer (three.js): three designs, 14 preset views, roof/floor toggles
- `3d/comparison.html`: 14 views with all three designs side by side; `3d/images/` holds the 42 renders
- `videos/`: 94-second walkthrough video of each design (MP4, 1280×720)
- `plans/`: measured floor plans for Plan 2 and Plan 5 (PDF/PNG/SVG) and a one-page shortlist sheet

## Notes
- The floor plans are measured from the original drawing; the 3D views are massing models built from those plans. Finishes, furniture and façade details are indicative.
- Concept layouts for discussion, not for construction. The architect must confirm sizes, structure, services and local by-laws.
- Location and personal details are intentionally left out.
